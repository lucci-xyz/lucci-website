# Newsletter brand image exports

These PNGs are reusable email assets for the Resend templates in `newsletter/`.

- `lucci-labs-lockup.png`: 750 × 228, transparent background; intended display size 200 × 61.
- `lucci-watermark.png`: 1320 × 360, pale wordmark on `#f7f6f2`; intended display size 360 × 98.

The symbol and Lucci wordmark paths come from [the existing brand master](https://github.com/lucci-xyz/luccihq/blob/main/components/lucci-logo.tsx), source blob `ef8eece5a4e59b289b3445e7063c58775ad75b0c`. The lockup adds the Labs descriptor in Arial. Colours and paper treatment follow [Lucci's brand guide](https://github.com/lucci-xyz/luccihq/blob/main/docs/brand.md).

Only brand geometry was rasterized; no research images, recordings, or private data are included. Keep the source master as the authority when regenerating these derivatives.
